About Me

Turning ideas into things people can click, read, and remember.

I'm Rajib — a developer and visual creator who likes projects that sit right at the edge of code and craft. Most days that means shipping clean, fast websites; some days it means being behind a camera, framing a shot that tells its own story.

I care about the details most people skip: load times, spacing, the way a hover feels. That habit carries over into everything else I make — presentations, videos, photos, and the odd side project that never quite ships on schedule.
